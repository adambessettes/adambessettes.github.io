Research interests
  • Interacting particle systems & mean-field limits: propagation of chaos, emergence of macroscopic laws, and statistical recovery of interaction mechanisms from data.
  • Optimal transport: variational structures and natural metrics for quantifying convergence in particle systems.
  • Stochastic control & filtering: Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equations, state estimation from noisy partial observations, and high-dimensional approximation.
Research projects
  • [WIP] Mean-Field Limits of Interacting Geometric Brownian Motions (Columbia University, supervised by Graeme Baker)
    • Studying N particles evolving as independent geometric Brownian motions subject to random merger and duplication jumps.
    • Formally derived the limiting empirical measure PDE (a Fokker-Planck equation with a coagulation convolution term) using generators and martingale arguments, and currently calibrating richer interaction kernels to real data.
  • Deterministic Control & Nonlinear Filtering (École Polytechnique, supervised by Philippe Moireau)
    • Proved the equivalence between Mortensen's minimum-energy observer and the Kalman filter in the linear-quadratic setting, and recast the nonlinear prediction step as an HJB equation.
    • Implemented vanishing-viscosity (heat-equation) and LogSumExp numerical solvers, benchmarked against the Extended Kalman Filter on linear and nonlinear systems.
